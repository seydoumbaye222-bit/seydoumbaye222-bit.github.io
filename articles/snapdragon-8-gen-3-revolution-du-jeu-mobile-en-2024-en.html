Snapdragon 8 Gen 3: Revolutionizing Mobile Gaming in 2024

← Retour à l'accueil

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, Qualcomm’s latest flagship, promises to transform the smartphone gaming experience with a hybrid architecture, a boosted GPU, and optimized thermal management, all in a context where mobile titles are becoming increasingly demanding.

CPU Architecture: a notable performance leap

The new chipset integrates a primary Cortex‑X4 core clocked at 3.3 GHz, surrounded by two Cortex‑A720 cores and three Cortex‑A520 cores, delivering up to 30 % more compute power compared to the Gen 2. This configuration reduces load times and improves the fluidity of high‑frame‑rate games, especially in multithreaded mode.

Adreno 820 X GPU: high‑fidelity graphics

The GPU increases from 18 to 20 compute units, reaching 1.5 teraflops, resulting in more detailed rendering, simplified ray‑tracing, and a stable 120 Hz refresh rate even on the most demanding titles such as Genshin Impact or Call of Duty Mobile.

Enhanced energy efficiency and thermal management

Thanks to a 4 nm process and the integration of a new dynamic cooling system, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 consumes roughly 20 % less power under sustained load while keeping the SoC temperature below 45 °C, thereby avoiding throttling that hampers long gaming sessions.

Improved compatibility and user experience

The chipset supports the latest Vulkan 1.3 standard, HDR10+, and Wi‑Fi 7, ensuring minimal latency and a stable connection for cloud gaming. Additionally, the integrated AI Engine optimizes textures and real‑time effects, providing increased immersion without sacrificing performance.

Conclusion

In 2024, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 marks a decisive step toward smoother, visually stunning, and more energy‑efficient mobile games, cementing Qualcomm’s position as a leader in mobile performance and paving the way for gaming experiences that increasingly approach console quality.
